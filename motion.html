The four rules

Principles before pixels

01Reveal, don't decorate. Every motion answers: what changed, where to look, is it working?
02One hero beat per view. The score gauge sweeps; everything around it stays quiet.
03Weighted, not floaty. A single settle-easing gives motion physical, trustworthy mass.
04Respect the user. Reduced-motion is a designed path. 60fps, transform/opacity only.

Accessibility

The reduced-motion path is designed, not disabled

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce (or the toggle up top), travel, loops and parallax are removed — but meaning stays: things still appear, states still confirm, the gauge jumps straight to its final value. Nothing is lost, only the vestibular load.

Motion onRise + fade, staggered, gauge sweeps, shimmer skeletons.
Motion offInstant opacity, no travel, gauge lands on value, static skeletons.
AlwaysFocus rings, color-coded state, and text labels — never motion-only meaning.
