02

About

Why we exist

We built Grandeza because "we think we're compliant" isn't a defense.

Every HVAC and plumbing shop we met had automated their customer intake — texts, reminders, AI phone agents — and none of them could prove it was legal. Not because they were careless. Because nobody built the tools to make compliance provable for the trades.

The TCPA doesn't care that you're a good operator. It cares whether you can produce signed consent, a Do-Not-Call check, and a clean record for every message you sent. Screenshots of a text thread won't do it — and the first time most shops think about any of this is the day a demand letter lands.

So we built the opposite of a rip-and-replace. Grandeza scores your exposure across 16 controls, hardens the tools you already run, and hands you a binder that says — on paper, signed — you did the work. It's the difference between hoping you're covered and proving it.

Grandeza provides compliance engineering, documentation and monitoring. We are not a law firm and we don't give legal advice. Many of our clients hand our binder straight to theirs.

FAQs

Do you replace my chatbot or phone system?
No. Hardening works with what you already run — Twilio, your CRM, your intake bot. We add the compliance layer around it. If you'd rather hand the whole intake off, Protected Intake is our turnkey option, but most shops start by hardening.
Is Grandeza a law firm?
No — Grandeza is compliance engineering. We build the controls, the audit trail and the documentation that demonstrate you did the work. We don't provide legal advice. Many clients hand our binder straight to their attorney.
What does it actually cost?
The risk scan is free. Hardening is $1,500 one-time plus Guardian from $149/mo. Protected Intake is $2,000 setup + $349/mo. Your free scan comes with a fixed-scope quote — no hourly surprises.
We've never had a complaint. Why now?
TCPA claims are filed by professional plaintiffs who target automated messaging — you don't get a warning. The point of the binder is that it exists before the demand letter. Documenting compliance after the fact is worth far less.
What if my score doesn't improve?
Our Clean-Score Guarantee: if a hardening engagement doesn't move every control to PASS, we keep working at no extra cost until it does. You reach "Protected," or you don't pay the balance.
