05 · Metadata & schema

Standards + structured data coverage

Titles ≤60 chars keyword-first; descriptions 140–155 chars; self-referencing canonical; OG/Twitter on every page. Drop-in JSON-LD ships in assets/schema/.

Organization + WebSite · site-wide Service + Offer · /product, /pricing FAQPage · pillars, landing HowTo · guides BreadcrumbList · deep pages Article · blog/clusters Review / AggregateRating · only when genuine

Integrity guardrail: never emit Review/AggregateRating markup for testimonials that aren't real, verifiable reviews — it breaks Google policy and risks a manual action. Validate every template in the Rich Results Test before shipping.
