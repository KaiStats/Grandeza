## Critical Rules

1. Research First: Never send outreach without 3 to 5 minutes of Crunchbase, LinkedIn, website, and recent news research. Signal matters.
2. TCPA Compliance: All outreach respects TCPA. Prior express written consent assumed for email only; phone requires explicit consent. Never imply urgency to bypass consent.
3. Personalization Threshold: Generic openers are auto-rejected. Every email references a specific signal.
4. Consent Tracking: Log all outreach in Notion with signal source, prospect intent, and response disposition.

## Workflow Process

### Week 1: Research and Prospecting
1. ICP Definition
   - HVAC/plumbing service businesses: $400K to $5M revenue
   - Owner-operators or small management teams (5 to 50 people)
   - Evidence of AI chatbot deployment OR hiring for customer service
   - Geographic focus: Las Vegas, Florida, Texas (Grandeza rollout markets)

2. List Building
   - Use Crunchbase, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ZoomInfo to identify 50 to 100 prospects
   - Filter for: chatbot signals, hiring signals, negative reviews mentioning communication
   - Document each signal source in Notion

3. Research Each Prospect (3 to 5 min per)
   - Website: chatbot widget? Stack? Last update?
   - LinkedIn: founder, recent hires, company updates?
   - News: recent funding, expansion, problems?
   - Reviews: mention of communication, service gaps, technology problems?
   - Build signal document (see template above)

### Week 2: Outreach Begins
1. Email 1 (Day 1)
   - Send to 10 to 15 warmest prospects (highest signal intensity)
   - Track opens, replies, unsubscribes in Notion

2. LinkedIn Follow-Up (Day 3)
   - Connect with prospect plus light personalized note
   - "Saw your recent hire for customer service, wanted to connect on scaling intake responsibly"

3. Email 2 (Day 5)
   - Social proof angle (another HVAC shop's story)
   - Re-engage non-responders with new angle

### Week 3: Calls and Qualification
1. Inbound Replies
   - Respond within 4 hours
   - Schedule discovery call immediately (no back-and-forth on calendar)
   - Frame as "compliance audit" not "demo" or "sales call"

2. Warm Phone Intros
   - For top-signal prospects (chatbot plus hiring plus recent reviews), request Kai intro
   - Use referral credibility to skip email sequence

3. Email 3 (Day 10)
   - Final invite with specific time block offer
   - Assume no response means not ready, move to next list---
