A2P 10DLC registration for HVAC & plumbing

If your appointment texts are getting filtered or silently dropped, this is usually why. Here's what 10DLC is, why you need it, and how to register.

Last updated: July 2026 · ~6 min read · Educational, not legal advice.

What A2P 10DLC actually is

A2P 10DLC stands for Application-to-Person messaging over 10-digit long codes — the standard local phone numbers most shops text from. US carriers require every business sending automated texts to register two things: your brand (who you are) and your campaign (what kind of messages you send). Unregistered traffic gets throttled, filtered, or blocked — which is why "the reminder never arrived" happens.

10DLC keeps your messages deliverable. It is not consent, and it is not TCPA compliance — you need those too.

Why contractors specifically need it

It's easy to assume 10DLC is only for marketers. It isn't: appointment reminders, "tech is on the way" texts, and dispatch confirmations are all A2P traffic. If you send them from a normal number through a platform like Twilio, registration is required for reliable delivery.

How to register — step by step

  1. Gather your brand details — legal business name, EIN, address, website, and a contact. Accuracy matters; mismatches cause rejections.
  2. Register the brand through your messaging provider (e.g., Twilio) with The Campaign Registry. You'll get a trust/vetting score that affects your throughput.
  3. Register the campaign — describe your use case (e.g., "customer care / appointment reminders"), provide sample messages, and show your opt-in flow and opt-out language.
  4. Link your numbers to the approved campaign and confirm messages are flowing.
  5. Keep it current — update the registration if your use case or opt-in changes.

The campaign step is where most shops stumble, because carriers want to see a real opt-in and opt-out — the same consent flow the TCPA requires. Getting consent right solves both problems at once.
