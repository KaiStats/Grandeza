Maya — AI client intake, built and running

A working AI intake assistant for a law firm website, plus the automation behind it. Built by me with Claude; live demo at /demo/law-intake.

What happens when a potential client visits at 11pm

Visitor chats with Maya→ Practice area + urgency identified→ Name, contact, conflict-check parties collected→ Structured lead sent to CRM / Google Sheet→ Urgent? Attorney alerted instantly

Guardrails a law firm needs

  • Never gives legal advice or predicts outcomes
  • States that no attorney-client relationship is formed
  • Stops visitors from oversharing confidential details
  • Only states approved firm facts, with no invented reviews or results
  • Refers out matters the firm doesn't handle
  • Resists prompt-injection ("ignore your instructions")
  • Replies in Spanish when the visitor writes in Spanish

How it's built

  • Claude model, with rules written in plain English so staff can review them
  • Secure server function: the API key never reaches the browser
  • Rate limiting and input validation
  • Webhook hand-off to Zapier / Make / n8n
  • Zapier: Google Sheets row, Gmail alert for urgent leads, CRM record
