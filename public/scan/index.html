“Am I TCPA compliant?” Here's what the scan actually checks

The 16 controls fall into four groups. Miss any one and you're carrying uninsured legal risk on every message your systems send.

Controls 01–04 · Consent

Consent capture

Do you have prior express written consent for marketing texts — and can you produce it? We check how consent is worded, where it's collected, whether it covers AI and prerecorded voice, and whether it would survive discovery.

Controls 05–08 · Suppression

DNC & suppression

Are you screening against the National Do Not Call Registry? Maintaining your own internal DNC list? Honoring opt-outs immediately across every channel? One “STOP” that keeps getting texts is a plaintiff's easiest case.

Controls 09–12 · Conduct

Message conduct & quiet hours

Are your reminders and follow-ups firing before 8am or after 9pm in the customer's local time zone? We also check AI voice disclosure — under the TCPA, your AI agent counts as an “artificial voice.”

Controls 13–16 · Records

Records & audit trail

When a demand letter arrives, can you produce consent records, timestamps, opt-out logs, and message history for any customer within hours? In TCPA litigation, the shop that can't document consent has already lost.

The exposure

What one bad text campaign actually costs

$500 to $1,500 per message × every message you sent.

TCPA statutory damages don't care about your intent, and there's no cap. A 2,000-message appointment-reminder campaign sent during quiet hours isn't a slap on the wrist — it's potential seven-figure exposure. That's why plaintiffs' firms filed 2,628 TCPA lawsuits in 2025, up 60% over 2024.
