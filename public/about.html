Why we exist

We built Grandeza because "we think we're compliant" isn't a defense.

Every HVAC and plumbing shop we met had automated their customer intake — texts, reminders, AI phone agents — and none of them could prove it was legal. Not because they were careless. Because nobody built the tools to make compliance provable for the trades.

The problem we saw

The TCPA doesn't care that you're a good operator.

It cares whether you can produce signed consent, a Do-Not-Call check, and a clean record for every message you sent. Screenshots of a text thread won't do it. And the first time most shops think about any of this is the day a demand letter lands.

What we do about it

We built the opposite of a rip-and-replace.

Grandeza scores your exposure across 16 controls, hardens the tools you already run, and hands you a binder that says — on paper, signed — you did the work. It's the difference between hoping you're covered and proving it.

We work with your stack

We tell you the truth
