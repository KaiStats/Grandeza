Pricing questions

What does the free scan include?
A 3-minute guided walkthrough of your live SMS, call and web intake, your Grandeza Score (0–100) with a control-by-control breakdown, your top three exposures ranked by cost, and a fixed-scope hardening quote. No card, no obligation.
Are there hourly or hidden fees?
No. Hardening is a fixed $1,500 engagement; your scan comes with the scope in writing. Guardian and Protected Intake are flat monthly rates.
What if my score doesn't improve?
Our Clean-Score Guarantee: if a hardening engagement doesn't move every control to PASS, we keep working at no extra cost until it does. You reach "Protected," or you don't pay the balance.
