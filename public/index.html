Straight answers

Questions operators actually ask.

Do you replace my chatbot or phone system?
No. Hardening works with what you already run — Twilio, your CRM, your existing intake bot. We add the compliance layer around it. If you'd rather hand the whole intake off, Protected Intake is our turnkey option, but most shops start by hardening.
How much of my team's time does this take?
The scan is 3 minutes with one person who knows your workflow. The 4-week hardening needs a few short check-ins — we do the engineering. No code freeze, no downtime for your dispatchers.
Is Grandeza a law firm?
No — Grandeza is compliance engineering. We build the controls, the audit trail and the documentation that demonstrate you did the work. We don't provide legal advice, and nothing here is a substitute for your attorney. Many clients hand our binder straight to theirs.
What does it actually cost?
The risk scan is free. Hardening is a $1,500 one-time engagement plus Guardian monitoring from $149/mo. High-volume shops that want us to run intake end-to-end choose Protected Intake at $2,000 setup + $349/mo. Your free scan comes with a fixed-scope quote — no hourly surprises.
We've never had a complaint. Why now?
TCPA claims are filed by professional plaintiffs who target automated messaging — you don't get a warning. The point of the binder is that it exists before the demand letter. Documenting compliance after the fact is worth far less than documenting it in advance.
What if my score doesn't improve?
Our Clean-Score Guarantee: if a hardening engagement doesn't move every control to PASS, we keep working at no extra cost until it does. You reach "Protected," or you don't pay the balance.
