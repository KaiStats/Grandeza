Am I TCPA compliant? Here's what the scan actually checks

The 16 controls fall into four groups. Miss any one of them and you're carrying uninsured legal risk on every message your systems send.

Controls 1–4

Consent capture

Do you have prior express written consent for marketing texts — and can you produce it? We check how consent is worded, where it's collected, whether it covers AI and prerecorded voice, and whether it would survive discovery. (The FCC's one-to-one consent rule was struck down in January 2025, but written-consent requirements for marketing messages are still fully enforced.)

Controls 5–8

DNC & suppression

Are you screening against the National Do Not Call Registry? Maintaining your own internal DNC list? Honoring opt-outs immediately across every channel? One "STOP" that keeps getting texts is a plaintiff's easiest case.

Controls 9–12

Message conduct & quiet hours

Are your reminders and follow-ups firing before 8am or after 9pm in the customer's local time zone? A wave of quiet-hours class actions is hitting small businesses right now. We also check AI voice disclosure — under the TCPA, your AI agent counts as an "artificial voice."

Controls 13–16

Records & audit trail

When a demand letter arrives, can you produce consent records, timestamps, opt-out logs, and message history for any customer within hours? In TCPA litigation, the shop that can't document consent has already lost.
